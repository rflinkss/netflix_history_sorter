📥 How to export your Netflix viewing history

  1. Go to netflix.com and sign in to your account
  2. Click your profile icon in the top-right → Account
  3. Under "Profile & Parental Controls", click your profile name
  4. Click Viewing activity
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Download all
  6. Netflix will generate NetflixViewingHistory.csv — import it above
